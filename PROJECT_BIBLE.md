# Executive Summary

Aegis AI is an enterprise-grade AI platform designed to transform complex space mission data into actionable intelligence.

The platform leverages IBM Granite foundation models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), modern AI engineering practices, and interactive data visualization to assist engineers, researchers, and mission planners in making faster, safer, and more informed decisions.

The project is being developed following enterprise software engineering standards, ensuring maintainability, scalability, security, and research readiness.
