# Business Requirements Specification

## Purpose

This document defines the business objectives, stakeholder expectations, business value, and success criteria for the Aegis AI platform.

It serves as the primary reference for aligning business goals with technical implementation.

## Business Problem

Space exploration organisations generate massive volumes of technical documentation, mission reports, telemetry summaries, satellite observations, and research papers.

These information sources are difficult to analyse efficiently.

Engineers spend significant time locating relevant knowledge before making decisions.

Current workflows rely heavily on manual analysis.

This increases operational complexity and slows decision-making.

## Business Opportunity

Artificial Intelligence enables organisations to transform technical information into actionable knowledge.

By combining IBM Granite foundation models with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Aegis AI can provide explainable recommendations, semantic search, and mission knowledge assistance.

The result is faster analysis, improved collaboration, and more informed decision-making.

## Business Goals

• Reduce mission information analysis time.

• Improve accessibility of technical knowledge.

• Increase operational efficiency.

• Demonstrate trustworthy AI.

• Build an enterprise-ready AI platform.

• Support future scientific research.

## Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Build an intelligent mission assistant.

- Improve knowledge retrieval.

- Generate explainable AI responses.

- Support engineering decision-making.

- Provide interactive dashboards.

- Integrate IBM Granite models.

## Stakeholders

Primary

• Mission Engineers

• Researchers

• Mission Planners

Secondary

• Students

• Universities

• IBM Judges

• Future Employers

• Open Source Community

## Business Value

The platform delivers value by:

• Reducing manual document analysis.

• Improving engineering productivity.

• Supporting evidence-based decision-making.

• Demonstrating enterprise AI engineering.

• Creating reusable AI infrastructure.

## Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if:

- AI responses are accurate.

- Documentation is complete.

- GitHub repository follows enterprise standards.

- User interface is responsive.

- IBM Challenge requirements are satisfied.

- Codebase is maintainable.

## Out of Scope

The following capabilities are intentionally excluded:

- Real spacecraft control

- Autonomous navigation

- Military applications

- Classified mission processing

- Real-time orbital control

## Business Risks

- Limited public datasets

- AI hallucinations

- API limitations

- Time constraints

- Model updates

- User adoption

## Expected Benefits

Short-term

- Successful IBM Challenge submission.

- Professional GitHub portfolio.

- Enterprise documentation.

Long-term

- Research publication.

- Resume enhancement.

- Expandable AI platform.

- Open-source community contributions.
