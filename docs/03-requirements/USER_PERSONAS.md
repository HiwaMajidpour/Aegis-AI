# User Personas

---

## Persona 1 — Mission Planner

### Profile

Mission planners coordinate space missions, evaluate operational risks, analyse mission objectives, and prepare mission execution strategies.

### Goals

- Reduce planning time
- Improve mission reliability
- Access technical knowledge quickly
- Receive AI-assisted recommendations

### Pain Points

- Large volumes of technical documentation
- Time-consuming analysis
- Fragmented information sources
- Difficulty identifying critical mission insights

### AI Needs

- Intelligent document search
- Mission summarisation
- Explainable recommendations
- Risk identification

### Expected Outputs

- Mission summaries
- AI recommendations
- Technical insights
- Decision support reports

## Persona 2 — Aerospace Engineer

### Profile

Engineers analyse spacecraft systems, investigate anomalies, and evaluate engineering reports.

### Goals

- Faster troubleshooting
- Technical knowledge retrieval
- Explainable AI assistance

### Pain Points

- Complex engineering documentation
- Knowledge scattered across reports
- High workload

### AI Needs

- Semantic search
- Engineering report analysis
- AI explanations
- Technical document comparison

### Expected Outputs

- Engineering summaries
- Suggested solutions
- Related documents
- Technical recommendations

## Persona 3 — Space Researcher

### Profile

Researchers study scientific publications, satellite observations, and mission datasets.

### Goals

- Accelerate literature review
- Discover knowledge connections
- Analyse research efficiently

### Pain Points

- Thousands of publications
- Manual knowledge extraction
- Difficult information synthesis

### AI Needs

- Research summarisation
- Citation assistance
- Knowledge retrieval
- Scientific question answering

### Expected Outputs

- Research summaries
- Key findings
- Relevant publications
- AI-generated insights

## Persona 4 — Student

### Profile

Students learn space science, mission planning, and AI technologies.

### Goals

- Learn faster
- Understand technical concepts
- Explore space missions

### Pain Points

- Difficult terminology
- Complex documents
- Limited learning resources

### AI Needs

- Educational explanations
- Interactive Q&A
- Simplified summaries

### Expected Outputs

- Easy-to-understand explanations
- Visual learning materials
- Educational recommendations
